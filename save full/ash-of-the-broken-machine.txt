Title: Ash of the Broken Machine

Lyrics:
Intro:
In the shadow of looming dread,
Smoke rises where promises bled.
Gears grind to a halt, silent and cold,
Ash rains down, stories untold.

Verse:
Whispers of betrayal linger in the dust,
Corroding the remnants left to rust.
Heartbeats of steel, now frozen in time,
Echo through the wasteland, a cold paradigm.

Pre-Chorus:
Iron dreams shatter, as night descends,
Where machinations falter and trust bends.
Faint are the cries of hope, doomed refrain,
Under the weight of ash and eternal pain.

Chorus:
In the ruin of the machine,
Where once we rose and dreamed,
Lies a world turned to ash,
Betrayed by those we deemed.
In the screams of silence found,
We rise from the ashes, unbound.

Breakdown:
Spirals of smoke choke the twilight,
The hum of forgotten engines, a distant blight.
Tangled wires, ghosts of intent,
In a kingdom of failure, we lament.

Outro:
Among ruins, we carve the path anew,
Through betrayal's shadow and aftermath's view.
Echoes of the past linger in dreams,
But from the ash, a new dawn gleams.

Music Prompt:
Create a doom metal soundscape with apocalyptic themes and intense industrial metal influences. Start with a slow, eerie intro featuring ethereal ambiance and distant mechanical clanks, gradually building into a massive wall of sound. The verse should be driven by sludgy, downtuned guitars, paired with a slow, pounding drum rhythm to convey the weight of despair and impending doom. For the pre-chorus, introduce tense, dissonant synths that weave through the oppressive atmosphere, heightening dramatic tension. The chorus must explode with a colossal sonic force—layer thick, distorted guitar riffs with a deep, rumbling bass, soaring melodic lines, and a powerful vocal delivery echoing the sense of betrayal and loss. Incorporate a breakdown with industrial noise elements, crashing percussion, and guttural growls to evoke the imagery of a machine collapsing into ruin. Conclude with an outro that reprises the haunting motifs of the intro, fading into a desolate soundscape that leaves listeners contemplating the aftermath.
